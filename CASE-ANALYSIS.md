## Main accounting conclusions

- September customer receipts of €90,000 are contract liabilities until delivery, not August revenue.
- The €50,000 bank receipt is borrowing, not income.
- The €60,000 packaging machine and €20,000 photo booth are PPE; the €10,000 repair is an expense.
- €70,000 villa spending and €40,000 owner-card spending are owner distributions.
- Write off the €18,000 R-17 receivable and recognise the €25,000 employee-claim provision.
- Write down €22,000 of water-damaged stock. The €2,000 disposal quote remains disclosure-only until a present obligation is evidenced.
- The primary profit is €74,000. It falls to €65,000 if the alternative €9,000 inventory movement figure is validated.

## Evidence limitations

The missing opening supplier ledger makes the €45,000 opening payable inferred rather than independently confirmed. The evidence supports no insurance expense; an amount must not be invented. Depreciation of €24,000 is a reasonable estimate but the detailed useful-life schedule is missing.
